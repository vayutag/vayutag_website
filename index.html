Frequently Asked Questions

Does the other person need an app? expand_more
No, the other person does not need an app. Modern smartphones have built-in NFC readers that automatically detect the card and open your profile in their default web browser.
Are there any monthly fees? expand_more
The basic digital profile and contact sharing features are completely free for life with your card purchase. We do offer premium plans for advanced analytics and enterprise features.
What phones are compatible? expand_more
Almost all modern smartphones (iPhone XR and newer, and most Androids from 2018 onwards) are fully compatible. For older phones, every card also includes a QR code on the back.
